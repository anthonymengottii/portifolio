## Overview

Landing page for **Pague Safe**, the payment gateway I co-founded and served as CTO. The platform offers solutions for e-commerce and infoprodutos: customizable checkout, multiple payment methods (PIX, credit card, boleto), antifraud system and 24/7 support. The company reached R$ 504 million in processed volume in one year. The page was built to present services, highlight benefits (security, fast withdrawals, high approval rates) and convert visitors into customers. Code available on [GitHub](https://github.com/anthonymengottii/paguesafe_landingpage). *Project managed during my tenure as CTO at Pague Safe.*

## Features and Sections

- **Hero**: Impactful conversion message and primary CTA
- **Partners**: Card brands and partner institutions (social proof)
- **Checkout**: Demonstration of customizable checkout
- **Resources**: Transparent integration, antifraud system, one-click buy, D+2 settlement, 24/7 support, multiple administrators
- **Pricing**: Fees and tariffs by payment method
- **Conversion**: Statistics and results
- **FAQ**: Frequently asked questions
- **CTA**: Exclusive fee negotiation
- **Footer**: Legal links and contact information

Additional pages: `/contato`, `/termos-de-uso`, `/politicas-de-privacidade`, `/termos-de-compra`, `/produtos-proibidos`, `/cookies`, `/espaco-do-consumidor`.

## Tech Stack

- **Next.js 15.1.2**: React framework for production
- **React 18**: User interfaces
- **TypeScript**: Static typing
- **Tailwind CSS**: Utility-first styling
- **Radix UI**: Accessible components
- **Vercel Analytics**: Performance metrics
- **Google Analytics & Tag Manager**: Tracking and analytics

## Challenges and Learnings
The main challenge was creating a landing page that effectively converts visitors into customers, requiring A/B testing and continuous optimization of elements such as CTAs, headlines, and forms.

Performance optimization was crucial, especially for loading on mobile devices, where most traffic occurs.

## Design and Outcome

Modern design with green/teal palette, clean and professional interface. Fully responsive (mobile, tablet, desktop), Next.js Image for image optimization, smooth animations and accessible components. Visual identity by **Dinamite Criativa**. Deploy optimized for Vercel. The first version of the landing had contributions from the **Anderson-Soares-Martins/pague-safe-project** repository.
