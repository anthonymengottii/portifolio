## About the Project

**Alpa** is an online payment processing platform offering solutions for businesses of all sizes: customizable checkout, multiple payment methods (PIX, credit card, boleto), antifraud system and 24/7 support. This landing page was built to present the company’s services, highlight benefits (security, fast withdrawals, high approval rates) and convert visitors into customers through an attractive, optimized experience. The project is live at [usealpa.com](https://usealpa.com) and the app at [app.usealpa.com](https://app.usealpa.com). Code available on [GitHub](https://github.com/anthonymengottii/alpa_landingpage).

## Features and Sections

- **Hero**: Impactful conversion message and primary CTA.
- **Partners**: Card brands and partner institutions (social proof).
- **Benefits**: Grid with main platform advantages.
- **Resources**: Highlight of available features.
- **Fees and Terms**: Information on fees and conditions.
- **Why users love Alpa**: Detailed benefit cards.
- **Fee Request**: Form for custom fee negotiation.
- **Get Started**: Final CTA for account creation.
- **FAQ**: Frequently asked questions in an interactive accordion.
- **Footer**: Legal links, contact and social media.

Additional pages: consumer space (`/consumidor`), contact (`/contato`), privacy policy, terms of use, purchase terms, prohibited products and cookie policy.

## Tech Stack

- **Next.js 15.2** (App Router): React framework for production and SEO.
- **React 19** and **TypeScript 5**: Typed interfaces.
- **Tailwind CSS v4**: Utility-first styling.
- **Framer Motion**: Animations and transitions.
- **Radix UI**: Accessible components (WCAG 2.1).
- **React Hook Form** and **Zod**: Forms and validation.
- **Zustand**: Lightweight state management.
- **Lucide React**: Icons. **Embla Carousel**: Accessible carousel.

## Performance and Results

Mobile-first, fully responsive design; Next.js Image for image optimization. The site achieves top scores on Google Lighthouse (Performance, Accessibility, SEO), ensuring fast loading on mobile networks. Visual identity by **Dinamite Criativa**. Deploy optimized for Vercel.
